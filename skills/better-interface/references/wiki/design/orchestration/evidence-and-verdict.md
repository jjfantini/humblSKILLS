## Evidence And Verdict

Consolidate root causes, require exact evidence, record rejected candidates, report verification gaps, and derive one final verdict.

### Review lens

- Cite exact source or rendered evidence for every finding.
- Consolidate repeated symptoms, document rejected candidates, and label checks that were not verified.
- Stay read-only by default; implement only on explicit request and verify again afterward.
