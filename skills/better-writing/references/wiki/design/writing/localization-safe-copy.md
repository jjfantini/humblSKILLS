## Localization Safe Copy

Prefer plain direct language, full pluralized templates, consistent capitalization, and placeholders that show format rather than replace labels.

### Review lens

- Prefer plain direct language, full pluralized templates, consistent capitalization, and placeholders that show format rather than replace labels.
- Review the complete user flow and nearby product terminology before proposing local copy changes.
- Report exact current copy and a complete replacement; preserve established voice when it remains clear.
